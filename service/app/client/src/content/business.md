## How ACoCo makes (and loses) money

**ACoCo Consulting** is a professional-services firm organised in six industry groups. Each group is run by a
**Partner MD** who owns a **portfolio of client work** (projects in delivery and deals being bid) and a **group of
people** (the consultants whose home cost centre is in the group).

**ACoCo sells people-hours.** Every consultant carries a **bill code** that sets what an hour costs and what it bills:

| Level | Bill code | Cost / day | Bill rate / day | Role in the pyramid |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Analyst | 30-39 | ~USD 300 | 620-800 | Builds |
| Consultant | 40-49 | ~USD 500 | 1,030-1,300 | Builds, leads small teams |
| Manager | 50-59 | ~USD 800 | 1,540-1,900 | Runs a team |
| Senior Manager | 60-69 | ~USD 1,000 | 2,050-2,500 | Owns a work package; co-leads bids |
| Principal | 70+ | ~USD 1,300 | 2,560+ | Leads Medium / Large projects and bids; one per group is the MD |

A project's economics are set by three levers: **the pyramid** (the bill-code mix on the team), **hours burned
versus hours sold** (most contracts are fixed price; overruns are lost margin and late delivery adds liquidated
damages) and **how much is built from scratch** (reusing a harvested asset delivers the same scope in fewer hours).

**The life cycle of an hour.** A deal is **bid** by a pursuit team booking hours to a pre-sales internal order -
cost with no revenue. A won deal becomes a **project**, staffed from people rolling off other work. At close the best
work is **harvested** into the asset library. People who roll off without a next assignment fall to the **bench** -
full cost, no revenue - until they are redeployed or put into **learning**.

## Five uses of time - deliberately unbalanced

| Category | Objective | Target by level (Analyst -> Principal) |
|---|---|---|
| **Delivery** | Maximise - it is the revenue | 75 / 71 / 60 / 45 / 35 % |
| **Bid** | Raise the win-rate, not the bid hours | 10 / 14 / 25 / 40 / 50 % |
| **Learning** | Shorten future delivery time | 10 % at every level |
| **Admin** | Keep below 5 % | 3 % |
| **Bench** | Drive to almost zero; ~2 % is structural transition | 2 % |

## Three pillars and the ProServe360 overlay

- **Operational Excellence** - deliver what was sold at the sold margin: CPI, SPI, forecast margin, asset reuse.
- **Value Creation** - win the right work: win-rate, pursuit cost per won USD, book-to-bill.
- **People Development** - the right people with the right skills: time mix vs target, bench, attrition, AI productivity.
- **ProServe360** joins the three: one risk register, one capacity picture, one advisor.

## Why Agentic AI matters - a hypothesis to test

AI-skilled consultants who actually use AI tools should deliver the same scope in fewer hours. That is a
**hypothesis the MD must test, not assume**: does a higher AI-skill level, and real use of AI tools, show up as higher
productivity (earned / actual hours) at the same level and work type? P6 answers it with a confidence interval.
